Bundled Novi Sad fallback photographs
======================================

The application shows these images only when the tablet has no locally stored
photos. Images downloaded to the tablet take priority.

The separately provisioned 100-photo Novi Sad cache has its own generated
ATTRIBUTIONS.txt and manifest.json in the local cache and on the tablet. Those
image binaries are intentionally not distributed through this repository.

Weather data
============
Foreca: https://www.foreca.com/
Weather API: https://developer.foreca.com/
Usage is subject to the account's Foreca terms and plan. The dashboard displays
Foreca attribution with the weather data. Existing application-drawn weather
icons are used; no Foreca preview images are bundled.
